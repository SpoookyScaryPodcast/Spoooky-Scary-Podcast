Behind the microphone

Curiosity is not a conspiracy.

Spoooky Scary Podcast digs into cryptids, UFOs and UAPs, creepypastas, government secrecy, cults, secret societies, and unexplained cases. Ghost stories may make an appearance, but the mission is bigger than things that go bump in the night: we follow the claims, examine the evidence, and explore the history behind the mystery.

Bring an open mind. Keep your critical thinking switched on. Not every mystery has an answer — and not every claim holds up.
